# Idea 01: The Surveillance Journey

## Outline
A character's journey through different forms of societal control. 

1. **Origin:** The protagonist begins in a rural area within a totalitarian country.
2. **The City:** The protagonist moves to a nice city, initially enjoying the amenities and modern life. However, they soon realize the intense surveillance state in place (similar to a social credit system in China).
3. **The "Free" Country:** Seeking freedom, the protagonist immigrates to a country boasting democracy and capitalism.
4. **The Realization:** Once there, they discover that while the government might not overtly control them, the people have voluntarily surrendered much of their freedom to networks and AI.
5. **The System:** The society operates on a peer-review-driven economy. People must provide reviews for every service to gain resources or money. Without realizing it, the citizens are constantly overseeing and policing each other, creating a decentralized form of surveillance.
